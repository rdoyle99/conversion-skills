# Verdict rules

- **Too early:** fewer than about 20 goal events in either window, or under 7 days after.
- **Worked:** the after-rate is clearly higher and holds on the second week.
- **No change:** within the usual week-to-week swing of the before-period.
- **Worse:** clearly lower; revert and record it.

Write the verdict with the counts: "sign-ups 14 of 610 visitors (2.3%) before, 23 of 640 (3.6%) after,
2026-10-01..10-07 vs 2026-10-08..10-14: too early, re-read on 2026-10-21."
