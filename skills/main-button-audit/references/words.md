# Button words

Say what happens next, in the visitor's words:
- "Start free" or "Try it free" when there is a free plan.
- "Book a demo" when a call is the next step.
- "Join the waitlist" when the product isn't open.

Weak: "Learn more", "Submit", "Contact" as the only main action. On 378 YC homepages (2026-10-28), "contact" was the
main button on 25.
