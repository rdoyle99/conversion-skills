# Scoring the first screen

The YC homepage check (2026-10-28) scored each site out of 100 and took off points per failed check. Use the same
weights so your score compares with theirs:

| Check | Points | YC share failing (2026-10-28) |
|---|---|---|
| Main button does nothing | 25 | 1% (2 of 298) |
| No main button on the first phone screen | 20 | 33% (122 of 366 where it could run) |
| Main button covered | 15 | 4% (11 of 290) |
| Page wider than a phone | 10 | 0% (0 of 378) |
| Main button too small to tap | 5 | 35% (95 of 268) |
| JavaScript errors from the site's own code | 5 | 5% (19 of 378) |
| Broken links | 5 | 3% (13 of 378) |
| Dead clicks | 5 | 10% (38 of 378) |
| Slow to load | 5 | 25% (96 of 378) |
| Images or files fail to load | 5 | 8% (29 of 378) |

The median YC score was 95 and 91 of 378 scored 100. Source: https://getuserproof.com/benchmarks/yc-homepages.
The headline 32% uses all 378 sites; the table's 33% uses the 366 where a phone screen could be read.
