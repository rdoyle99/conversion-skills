# Worked example: one product's plans from code

UserProof's plans, read from its plan file on 2026-10-20: Free covers 3 sites and 5,000 recorded visits a month;
Pro is $29 a month for 50,000 visits; Scale is $99 a month for 250,000. Past a plan's visits it keeps recording 1
in 4 visits until the month resets. A pricing page for these plans passes the checklist when each of those numbers
appears with its billing period and the past-the-limit sentence is on the page.
