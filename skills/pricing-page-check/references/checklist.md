# Pricing page checklist

- [ ] Prices match code or billing (file: ____, read YYYY-MM-DD)
- [ ] Every price has "billed monthly" or "billed annually" beside it
- [ ] Limits named in the buyer's unit, with what counts as one
- [ ] What happens past the limit is stated
- [ ] Free plan or trial is visible, with its limits
- [ ] Prices readable on a 390px phone without a tap
- [ ] Each plan's button works on a phone
- [ ] Currency shown
- [ ] FAQ answers: can I cancel, do I need a card, what happens to my data
